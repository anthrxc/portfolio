Who are you?

Thank you for asking!

I'm an 18-year-old freelance developer from Croatia.

I always had an interest in creating something of my own and sharing it with the world, so programming was the perfect combination with my existing interest in technology. My coding journey started around the time of COVID lockdown, when I couldn't do much apart from sit at home and cope with online school.

Since then, I have turned many of my weird and (at least for me) interesting ideas into reality with JavaScript. Later, I started experimenting with website design and development which is also a fascinating endeavour to me. Besides those, I am proficient in creating and maintaining MySQL or MongoDB databases, so whatever it is that a project requires, I can assure you I am capable of it.

When I'm not working on any projects, you'll either find me in a gym, or behind a good book.
Other than those, I like to watch Formula 1 whenever it's a race weekend, or simracing when it isn't.

I'm always open for new projects and ideas, so if you have anything in mind, feel free to contact me below!

What can you show me?

EuroFinance, Ltd.

As part of a school project, this is a pure-HTML website built for an imaginary bank. Over a couple of days, I created a multi-page website detailing the bank, it's services and the team behind it. As a result of my efforts, the website was praised as the best in class.

Bela+

Built with Node.js, Bela+ is a CLI program designed to track points in the card game Belote. Created with the idea to help out my friends when we'd play the game, and also to participate in a programming competition, Bela+ was a great challenge in figuring out the logic in the points system of the game. Although the competition didn't go as planned, my friend group enjoyed the program.

Passman CLI

What started as a learning experience for I/O in the command-line with Node.js, Passman soon turned into a passion project to challenge myself in craeting a password manager. Although it is local-only, it was still a great experience in learning how to communicate with the end user and store the provided information.
